Requirements recorded from this source:
- R005 (official_eikamet_channel): Use the official e-İkamet channel for the online application.
- R007 (application_form_signed): The generated residence-permit application form must be signed before it counts as ready.
- R009 (biometric_photo_current): The biometric photo must be within 6 months and ICAO-compliant. Lower bound 0 reproduces the milestone-1 engine (a photo age cannot be negative).
- R010 (residence_fee_evidence): Required residence-permit/document-fee payment evidence must be present. Exact amounts are dynamic. Do not hard-code TL amounts.
- R017 (address_proof_sabanci): For Sabancı operational submission, accepted address proof is dorm certificate, rental agreement, or notarized undertaking; Airbnb alone is not accepted. Government also recognizes other cases; outside this scoped workflow they should trigger manual verification.
- R019 (declaration_control_document): Sabancı declaration/control document must be present and signed.
- R020 (core_file_complete): The core Sabancı submission file must be complete before physical IRO submission.
- R021 (iro_remote_preverification): Scanned documents must be sent to IRO and IRO confirmation received before physical submission.
- R022 (fingerprint_gi_required): After online application, fingerprint/GI processing and a GI number are required in the Sabancı workflow.
- R023 (iro_submission_dependencies): Physical IRO submission is allowed only after online application, complete/preverified file, and GI/fingerprint requirements.
- R024 (current_submission_window): Sabancı submission must occur inside a currently published IRO window; actual dates must be refreshed. current_date is the evaluation date (derived 'today'); the window is iro_window_start..iro_window_end.
- R025 (application_form_current_window): Application forms from previous months/windows are not accepted for the current IRO submission window.
- R026 (missing_documents_block_iro): Sabancı IRO does not accept files with missing required documents.
- R028 (migration_is_final_authority): Final evaluation/approval belongs to Migration Management, not Sabancı IRO.
- R029 (track_after_submission): After IRO submission, application status should be tracked through official e-İkamet.
