- Foreigners who will stay in Türkiye longer than visa or visa exemption or who will extend their existing residence permits must apply for one of the residence permits
specified in Article 30 of the Law No. 6458 on Foreigners and International Protection.

residence permit suitable for the purpose of stay, and the residence permit applications of those who cannot justify the purpose of stay will be evaluated negatively in
accordance with the provisions of the legislation.
- Additional information or documents may be requested by Provincial Directorates of Migration Management in the evaluation of residence permit applications.
- Sanctions will be imposed on those who provide false information or statements within the scope of the relevant legislation provisions.
